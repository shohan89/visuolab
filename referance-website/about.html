How we think

The principles behind the work

Five things we won't compromise on, whatever the brief.

01

Craft over volume

We take on a handful of projects at a time so senior people stay on yours from kickoff to launch.

02

One idea, every surface

Brand, product and web are designed by the same hands, so the story doesn't drift between them.

03

Motion is meaning

Concepts, prototypes and working files replace slide decks. You see the work as it happens.

Launch is a milestone, not the finish line. We stay to measure, learn and iterate.

Our story

From two laptops in Lisbon to a studio on four continents

  1. 2017

    Two designers, one desk

    Visuolab starts as a two-person brand studio in a shared Lisbon workspace. First client: a seed-stage fintech that is still with us.

  2. 2019

    Product joins brand

    The first product-design retainer turns a brand shop into a full design studio. Five people, first Clutch reviews.

  3. 2021

    Remote by design

    We go fully distributed, with teammates in Toronto and Singapore, and become a Webflow professional partner.

  4. 2023

    The hundredth launch

    Design engineering and motion become core disciplines. First Awwwards Site of the Day.

  5. 2026

    Today

    Fourteen people across four continents, 140+ launches — and still no account managers between you and the work.

Why Visuolab exists

We started Visuolab because we were tired of watching good ideas get diluted between the deck, the design and the build. So we built a studio where the same people carry an idea from the first sketch to the last screen — and stay accountable for how it performs. Small on purpose. Senior by default. Honest about what will and won't move the needle.
